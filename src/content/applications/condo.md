---
title: Condominiums
description: Fresh air and humidity control for Singapore condo units, planned around the false ceiling and MCST rules.
order: 1
problem: Condo bedrooms are often kept shut with the aircon running. Humidity drops while the aircon is on, then climbs back once it is switched off, and CO₂ builds up overnight because little outdoor air gets in. Wardrobes and store rooms with no airflow pick up a musty smell.
families: [fresh-air-dehumidifiers, ceiling-dehumidifiers]
setup:
  - One fresh-air ceiling dehumidifier above the corridor or bathroom false ceiling
  - Short ducts to each bedroom and the living area
  - Outdoor air intake through an existing opening where the MCST allows it, or a new one if approved
  - Drain line tied into the nearest condensate or floor trap
---

Most condo renovations already include a false ceiling along the corridor, which is usually the easiest place for the unit. Check your MCST's renovation guidelines early, especially for any new wall openings or changes to the façade.
