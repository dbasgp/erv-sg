---
title: Schools and classrooms
description: Fresh air for air-conditioned classrooms, enrichment centres and childcare centres in Singapore.
order: 4
problem: A closed classroom with 20 to 40 people builds up CO₂ quickly once the doors are shut and the aircon is on. Opening the windows lets in heat, noise and humidity.
families: [erv-systems]
setup:
  - Ceiling-mounted ERV per classroom or per pair of rooms
  - Supply and exhaust grilles placed to avoid short-circuiting the airflow
  - Optional CO₂ sensor to adjust fan speed with occupancy
---
