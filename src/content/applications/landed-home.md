---
title: Landed homes
description: Whole-house dehumidification and fresh air for terrace, semi-detached and bungalow homes in Singapore.
order: 2
problem: Landed homes have more exterior walls, roof area and ground contact than an apartment, so they take on more moisture. Basements and ground-floor rooms tend to feel damp, and closed-up upper floors still need fresh air.
families: [erv-systems, ceiling-dehumidifiers, fresh-air-dehumidifiers]
setup:
  - An ERV or fresh-air dehumidifier per floor, sized to that floor's rooms
  - A separate ceiling dehumidifier for the basement, store or wine room if there is one
  - Ducts run in the false ceiling of each storey
  - Each zone with its own controller
---

During an A&A or rebuild, plan duct routes and drain points with the M&E design, not after the ceilings are closed up.
