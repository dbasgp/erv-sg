---
title: Hotels and serviced apartments
description: Fresh air and humidity control for hotel rooms and serviced apartments in Singapore.
order: 6
problem: Rooms left empty with the aircon off between guests can turn damp and pick up odours. Guests then switch the aircon to its coldest setting to make up for it.
families: [fresh-air-dehumidifiers, erv-systems]
setup:
  - Fresh-air dehumidifier or ERV per room, or per riser for larger buildings
  - Low fan speed while the room is unoccupied to keep it dry
  - Units placed above the entrance corridor ceiling for maintenance access
---
