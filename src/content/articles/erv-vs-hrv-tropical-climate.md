## Outline

1. What both machines do: balanced fresh air in, stale air out
2. The difference: an HRV moves heat only, an ERV moves heat and part of the moisture
3. Singapore's problem is moisture as much as heat, all year round
4. Where an HRV can still make sense (e.g. spaces with separate dehumidification)
5. What an ERV does not do: it reduces the moisture coming in, but it is not a dehumidifier
6. Pairing an ERV with a dehumidifier
7. Questions to ask before choosing
