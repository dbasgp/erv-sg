## Outline

1. When to bring it up: at layout stage, before ceiling and M&E drawings are fixed
2. Where the unit goes: corridor, bathroom or store room false ceiling
3. Ceiling depth and access panel size
4. Duct routes and grille positions
5. Drain point and fall
6. Power point and controller location
7. MCST approval for any new openings
8. Handover: filter access, servicing
